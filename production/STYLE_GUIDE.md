# Style guide
Current default: V1. Brand: Hidjaz Premium, premium Quran product advertising.
- Story first: opening hook, authentic product benefits, clear call to action when supplied.
- Avoid misleading promises, fabricated product details, artificial product changes.
- Clean natural cuts, elegant restrained motion, varied visual layouts per video.
- Accurate Kyrgyz or Russian subtitles, reviewed against actual speech; maintain safe margins.
- Voice clear, noise reduction moderate, avoid robotic sound. Music only on explicit approval and licensed assets; duck beneath voice.
- No automatic paid TTS or external paid tools.
- Default vertical output 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264/AAC; respect source format.
- Future styles: add V2/V3 sections and record selected version per job. Do not retroactively change existing jobs.

## Required output container
Regardless of whether the input is .MOV or .MP4, deliver the edited final video as .mp4 with H.264/AAC and yuv420p. Never rename MOV to MP4 without real encoding/remuxing. Check video/audio synchronization and playback before marking done.
